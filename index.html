About this survey

We are investigating how to distinguish acceptable variation in target contours from differences that require correction. This survey asks what that range should mean, how it could be constructed, and what evidence would support its use.

The statements are proposals to test, not established recommendations. Agreement and disagreement are equally useful. The focus is target volumes; organs at risk are considered through the effects of target contour changes on toxicity.

There are two planned rounds. Round 2 will provide anonymised group results for consideration as a group. You will then rate the statements again. Differences of opinion remaining after Round 2 will be reported.

How to respond

  1. Statements are shown one at a time, section by section.
  2. Rate each statement independently, as written. If it depends on a particular site, target type or clinical setting, explain that in the comment box.
  3. Choose U when you cannot judge a statement.
  4. Comments are optional and welcome: caveats, conditions, or other perspectives to consider.
  5. You can review and change any answer before submitting. Your answers are kept in this browser as you go, so you can close the page and return on the same device and browser.

Rating scale

A neutral view (3) and inability to judge (U) will be analysed separately.
